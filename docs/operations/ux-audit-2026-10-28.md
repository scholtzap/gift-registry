# UX audit — user workflows, 2026-10-28

Method: Nielsen heuristic walkthrough (new user, then experienced user, then each
task flow) against the live deployment at 390×844 (WhatsApp in-app browser size)
with Playwright, plus the signed-in owner/friend flows against a locally seeded
fictional cast. Screenshots were captured for every step (scratch:
`%TEMP%\audit-shots\`, fictional data only). Severity: 4 catastrophe, 3 major,
2 minor. Change requests are the `ux-*` plans in `docs/plans/`.

The reported bug reproduces exactly: on `https://pinata.scholtz.work/app`,
clicking **Open Piñata** leaves the URL unchanged
(`OPEN_PINATA before=…/app after=…/app SAME=true`). It is a link to the page
you are already on.

## Workflows audited

| # | Persona | Journey | Verdict |

## Findings

### Severity 4 — catastrophe

- **F1 · “Open Piñata” reloads the page.** The app landing's only primary
  action is `href="/app"`, which under the single-domain deployment *is* the
  landing. Heuristics: consistency, error prevention. (Reproduced live.)
- **F2 · There is no registration flow for the birthday person.** A new owner
  can only (a) message a WhatsApp bot that is not live yet, or (b) be created
  *for* by a friend through the proxy flow. Nobody can sign up, create their
  own circle, and get a link to share. The site's “Start your circle” promise
  has no matching workflow.
- **F3 · The creation entry (`/lookup` and the proxy form) renders as bare
  unstyled HTML** — no shell, no CSS, no brand. It is the first form a new
  organiser touches and it looks like a broken page from 1998. Heuristics:
  aesthetic integrity, trust.

### Severity 3 — major

- **F4 · Broken screenshot strip on the app landing.** Three broken-image
  icons and visible development copy (“Captured from the seeded demo
  (tests/browser/screenshots.spec.ts)”). The capture spec exists and is
  opt-in; the images were never committed.
- **F5 · `/app/app` is a dead end.** “Use your invitation link” with no field to
  paste a link, no find-by-number, no create option. Anyone who arrives
  without the exact link stops here.
- **F6 · Three landing pages in a row.** Site `/` → app `/` → app `/app` each
  re-tell the story; the CTA is renamed at every hop (Start your circle →
  Open Piñata → use your invitation link). One door should do this job.
- **F7 · Proxy flow explains itself too late.** “Type their number and we
  will help you start” does not say you are creating a circle *for someone
  else*, that they never need to join, or why your own name/number come next.
- **F8 · Join-gate failure has weak recovery.** “We could not let you in with
  that number.” is accurate but the way out (Ask to be added) is a same-weight
  secondary button, and the form does not say what to check first (country
  code? the number the organiser used?).

### Severity 2 — minor

- **F9 · Owner date form always demands the date**, even with a date already
  set and counting down. Pre-fill it and make “Save the date” an update.
- **F10 · In-app brand and privacy links escape the app** (`/` and `/privacy`
  on the app screens land on the marketing host's pages, a different privacy
  notice from `/app/privacy`). Home also drops the user's circle context.
- **F15 · Copy says “The app is live” while the deployment is DEMO_MODE** with
  one seeded circle. A small “demo” marker on the app landing would keep the
  promise honest until live WhatsApp lands.
- **F16 · The guide's first owner step is stale** (“Sign up on the home page…
  we email it”) now that the CTA goes to the app. Update when F2 ships.
- **F14 · Information density:** the app landing repeats the site's how-it-works
  almost verbatim. If F6 collapses the doors, this resolves itself.

## What already works well (keep)

- Owner and friend ledgers are clear, well-typed, and the two-projection
  story reads instantly (“OWNER VIEW / FRIENDS ONLY” stickers).
- Settings explains each AI capability in plain words with its manual fallback.
- The join gate's privacy copy (“We check the invite list. This does not verify
  that you control the number.”) is honest and precise.
- The card flow keeps the reveal a human act everywhere. No change requested.
