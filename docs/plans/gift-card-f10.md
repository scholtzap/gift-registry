# Gift card (F10)

**Status:** Decided into v1 on day 5–6 of the ten-day plan. Everything below is the build shape; the
product decision and its rationale live in `SPEC.md` §Decisions (F10) and the "reveal is a human act"
rule in `AGENTS.md`.

## Problem

The ledger tracks the gift but nothing is handed over. Every flow in v1 ends at "the pool is
`purchased`" — a state in a table. The origin transcript's actual promise is social: it takes the
awkwardness out of asking *and* gives the group something to physically attach to the box. The card is
also the closing beat the 3-minute demo lacks after the money shot at 2:05, and it adds a fourth Meta
surface (Spark for the pun, optionally `muse-image` for the background) to a rubric that scores Use of
Meta AI tooling at 25.

## What the card contains

- The item (title, image if we have one, retailer omitted deliberately).
- The recipient's first name and the occasion.
- A pun/one-liner generated by Spark, chosen by the assigned buyer from three candidates.
- **Every contributor's first name plus their own one-line message** — this is the part the user asked
  for and the reason the card is a group artefact rather than a receipt.
- No amounts. A keepsake that lists who paid what becomes a financial document, and nobody asked.

## Decisions specific to the card

| Choice | Why | Rejected alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Contributor writes their own message, stored verbatim | Their words are the value; a model paraphrasing a friend's message is the product lying about a person | Model-written messages per contributor |
| `anonymous` opt-out per contributor, first name shown by default | Not everyone in the group wants their name on a printed artefact | Forced attribution |
| URLs rejected on write, 200-char cap | A message field that accepts a link is a channel that can smuggle a retailer or a cheaper price into the owner's hands, defeating the deals-are-friends-only rule | Free text with sanitising at render time |
| Text rendered by us (SVG → PNG), model output is copy only | Printable text must be exact; generated-image typography is unverified and a typo on a printed card is a visible failure | Asking `muse-image` to typeset the card |
| `muse-image` supplies only a background/object illustration layer, cached, optional | Earns the image-model line without making correctness depend on it | Illustration as the card body |
| Card composed from `card_entries` rows, not one stored blob | A contributor can add or withdraw their line without regenerating history, and the row count *is* the "who contributed" query | Single JSON column of names |

## The privacy consequence (this is the reason the feature is not trivial)

The circle link lives in a WhatsApp group **that contains the owner**. WhatsApp builds a link preview
server-side and shows it to every member. So the obvious, attractive design — make the card image the
`og:image` of the share link because it is a beautiful shareable artefact — would render contributor
names **for the owner**, past RLS, past the view builder, past role-scoped agent toolsets. That design
was proposed during the ideation pass and is explicitly revoked here.

Hard requirements, each of which becomes a leak-suite row (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §8):

1. `/cards/:id` and `/api/cards/:id.png` are friend-role routes. Owner session → **404, never 403**
   (403 confirms a card exists, which is itself the leak).
2. No `og:`/`twitter:` tags on any card route; `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`; `Cache-Control: private, no-store`.
3. The circle share link's preview must be static brand art that never contains contributor data.
4. The system never sends the card anywhere. Download is the only delivery; forwarding is the
   gifter's deliberate act of revealing.
5. RLS: no read policy for `app_owned` on `card_entries` or `gift_cards`.
6. The pun is generated from (item title, recipient first name, occasion) only — never from messages,
   so a generated string cannot carry a contributor's words into an owner-visible surface.
7. A contributor message is **untrusted text that reaches a renderer**. Escape at SVG build time, and
   strip `<script>`, `foreignObject` and any external `href`/`xlink:href` from SVG before rasterising —
   a headless HTML→PNG converter that resolves remote references turns one friend's message into an
   SSRF probe from your own VM. Prefer escaping text into `<text>` nodes over ever feeding raw user text
   to a browser-based renderer.

## Build shape (day 5–6, ~half a day)

- `server/db/` — `card_entries`, `gift_cards` tables, expand-only migration, RLS policies added in the
  same file as the rest of the policy set.
- `app/(friend)/items/[id]/card/` — friend-only route; contributor line editor with own-preview;
  buyer picks the pun.
- `lib/cards/copy.ts` — Spark call, flat `json_schema` `{pun, one_line, alternate}`, result cached on
  `gift_cards` (a retried call must not create a second card: `item_id UNIQUE`).
- `lib/cards/render.ts` — deterministic SVG composition → PNG at A6 300 DPI with bleed, and a screen
  variant. Scales 1–8 contributor names; a solo claim renders a different layout, not a group layout
  with one name in it.
- `lib/cards/art.ts` — optional `muse-image` background, generated once per occasion and cached as a
  committed asset, never per-card at runtime.
- Leak suite: the four card surfaces above, asserted with the role the test *claims* it set.

## Acceptance

- A pool member downloads a card naming every contributor with their own message; the assigned buyer's
  name is marked as the buyer.
- The owner's session on the same URL gets 404, and the owner's item sheet contains no affordance that
  leads there.
- Pasting the circle link into a real group containing the owner shows a preview with no contributor
  name (manual check, day-9 checklist item 7).
- A tombstoned item with a funded pool still renders its card — the keepsake belongs to the gifters.
- Card copy records `model_used`, so "the pun came from Spark" is evidenced in the ingest log, not
  asserted.

## Kill criterion

If day 6 ends without the renderer working, ship the **text-only card** (HTML, print stylesheet, no
PNG pipeline, no generated art) — the names and messages are the feature, the file format is not. If
the leak rows cannot be made green, the card is cut entirely; it is the only optional feature on the
light days and `SPEC.md` already says features go before nothing that touches privacy.

## Out of scope

Email/SMS/WhatsApp delivery of the card · recipient photo or any generated likeness of a real person
(Meta Automated Data Collection + POPIA) · amounts on the card · physical print fulfilment · card
templates marketplace · letting the owner see or reveal a card.
