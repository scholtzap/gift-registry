How it works

You pick the wishes. Your friends plan the rest.

Everything happens in the browser. Share one circle link; friends claim, pool, and write the card there.

  1. 1

    Make a circle and add wishes

    Open the app, create your circle, and type each wish with a price, shop and photo if you have one. Check every draft before it goes on your list.

  2. 2

    Share one link

    Use Share this link on your circle page, or copy the address into your group chat. That one link is the invite.

  3. 3

    Friends plan; someone hands it over

    Friends claim a gift or pool behind one buyer, mark what they paid, and write card messages. Someone downloads the card and hands it over themselves. Piñata never sends it.

Questions

Before you start

Does Piñata hold our money?
No. Friends pay each other directly. Piñata only records who pledged what and who has marked it paid.
Which currencies does it use?
US dollars by default. You can price each gift in rand, euro or pounds instead, and a pool always uses its gift's currency.
Do I need to install an app?
No. Piñata opens in your browser. Create a circle, then share its link with your group.
How do friends join?
They open the circle link you shared, enter the name they want on the gift card, and the number that was invited. If they are not on the list yet, they can ask and you let them in.
