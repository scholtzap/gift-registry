Privacy notice

Last updated 4 November 2026

This notice covers this site, gifts.scholtz.work, and the Piñata app at pinata.scholtz.work. Piñata is a group gift list you use in the browser: one person keeps a wish list, shares one circle link with their group, and friends plan who buys what without the list owner seeing it.

What we collect

Contact form: your email, your message, your name if you add it, and the time you sent it.

In the app, about you: the name you want on gift cards, the email or phone you use to log in to your circles dashboard, and the WhatsApp number friends join against. Numbers on a circle's invite list are stored only as a keyed hash.

Gift lists: wishes, prices, shop names, product photos, and the sizes and favourites the list owner chooses to share with friends.

Friends' plans: who claimed or is buying each gift, pools, pledges, "paid" marks, bank details a buyer chooses to share with pledgers, secret gift ideas and card messages.

Product photos: a photo you attach to a gift in the browser. We save a resized copy and remove its location, camera and other metadata first.

Optional WhatsApp intake: if you message the Piñata WhatsApp number, we store the voice notes, screenshots or links you send so they can become a draft you check in the app before anything is added. You can switch each of those features off in Settings.

Who can see it

The list owner sees their own wishes, sizes and photos. They do not see claims, pools, pledges, who is buying what, secret ideas, card messages or photos friends add to those ideas.

Friends in a circle see the wishes, the shared sizes, who is buying each gift, pool progress, each other's plans and the photos on gifts they can see. People outside the circle see nothing.

We do not sell this data and we do not show ads.

Who processes it for us

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Contact form messages are emailed to us through Resend.

The app runs on our own host. Circle data and photos are kept in our own database and file storage.

If you use optional WhatsApp intake, those messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. When you leave an AI setting on, Meta's models may read the voice note, image or link to draft a gift. Switch a setting off in the app and that path stops for you.

This site sets no cookies and loads no analytics, ads or third-party fonts. Sign-in uses a short email code; the session cookie is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax.

Keeping and removing it

We keep contact messages while they are useful for replying. We keep circle data and photos until you ask us to remove them. Ask through the contact form and we will delete your details and the photos you added. Card messages and pledges other people wrote stay with them.

South African users also have rights under POPIA. Send any request through the contact form and we will reply within a reasonable time.

Contact

Use the contact form, or open My circles to reach your circles dashboard.
